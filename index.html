About 2048 — Curated Studio Edition (v2.0.1)

Originally inspired by Gabriele Cirulli's 2014 classic, 2048 is a deceptively simple game of exponential growth ($2^1$ through $2^{11}$ and beyond). Every slide shifts all tiles across the grid; colliding tiles of identical rank fuse into the next power of two, adding the newly forged tile's value to your score.

🎮 Game Modes

  • Classic Endless: Traditional rules (90% 2, 10% 4 spawns). Win at 2048 and keep going to 4096+.
  • ⏱️ 2-Min Time Blitz: Race against a 120-second clock to rack up the highest score before time expires.
  • 🧘 Zen Relax: Stress-free mode with unlimited Undos, Shuffles, and Zaps — never get locked out!
  • 🔥 Hardcore Purist: 25% 4 spawn rate with Undo, Hints, AI, and Power-Ups disabled.

⚡ Power-Ups & Streaks

  • 🔥 Merge Streak: Consecutive moves that produce at least one merge build your Streak counter.
  • 🔀 Shuffle (X): Rearranges all existing tiles into new positions when your board is gridlocked.
  • ⚡ Zap Low (C): Vaporizes one lowest-ranking tile on the board to open breathing room.
  • Bonus Charges: Forging a 512, 1024, or 2048 tile awards +1 charge (max 3).

🧠 Grandmaster Strategy Guide

  1. Pick One Corner & Lock It: Choose a corner (e.g., Top-Left) for your highest tile. Restrict yourself to 3 directions (e.g., Up, Left, Right) and avoid pressing Down unless forced.
  2. Build a Snake Chain: Arrange descending values in a serpentine path (2048 → 1024 → 512 → 256 along the top row, then snaking into the second row) so cascades happen in sequence.
  3. Keep Your Anchor Row Packed: Fill all 4 cells of your anchor edge before sliding parallel to it so a newly spawned 2 cannot slip into your corner.
  4. Save Power-Ups for Endgame: Hold your Zap and Shuffle charges for when forging 1024 → 2048 leaves low tiles trapped between giants.
