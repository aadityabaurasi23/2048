About 2048 — Studio Edition (v2.4.0)

Slide tiles across the grid using arrow keys, WASD, or touch swipes. Matching tiles fuse into the next power of two (2¹ → 2¹¹), emitting ambient shockwaves onto your theme-connected 60 FPS Live Wallpaper.

🧠 Grandmaster Strategy

  1. Corner Anchor: Lock your largest tile in one corner and keep its edge row filled.
  2. Snake Chain: Build descending ranks in a serpentine line so merges cascade smoothly.
  3. Smart Power-Ups: Save 🔀 Shuffle, ⚡ Zap, and 🔨 Upgrade for endgame gridlock; forging 512+ awards bonus charges!
